03 · The Path

How I got here

In order — each stage built on the last.

01

The spark, with nowhere to put it

I wanted this at fourteen. I read everything I could find on ethical hacking and red teaming and had no way to act on a word of it — no resources, no guidance, no idea what a path into the field even looked like. So it stayed something I read about.

02

The detour

I finished high school and took a diploma in mechanical engineering — the option in front of me, not the one I wanted. I spent it on a subject I had no interest in and came out the other side assuming I'd missed my window.

03

A way back in

Then I found an institute near me teaching cybersecurity and took the chance — a BCA in Cloud & Security alongside a Diploma in Cybersecurity. I gave the first three or four months entirely to fundamentals: Linux, networking, Python, before touching anything offensive. TryHackMe came next, to push further on my own time.

January 2026
04

It clicked

CEH is where it connected. The theory finally had somewhere to attach and everything I'd been drilling made sense at once — after that, studying stopped feeling like studying, and I wanted all of it. 370 rooms, a self-hosted VMware lab with a Windows domain I could break and rebuild, and a writeup for every box worth one.
